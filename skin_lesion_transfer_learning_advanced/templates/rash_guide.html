Rash & Allergy
Learning Guide

A rash may have many causes. This page gives general information only and deliberately does not guess an allergy from a photo.

Seek urgent help for emergency symptoms

Get emergency medical help if a rash comes with trouble breathing, swelling of the lips/tongue/throat, faintness, or rapidly worsening severe symptoms. For new, persistent, painful, spreading or concerning rashes, talk to a healthcare professional.

COMMON POSSIBILITIES

Visible rash does not equal allergy

EXAMPLEContact irritationCan occur after contact with soaps, fragrances, cleaning products or other irritants.
EXAMPLEEczemaCan cause dry, itchy, inflamed skin; several factors may contribute.
EXAMPLEInfection or other causesSome rashes are related to infections or other conditions and need professional assessment.

Safe next steps

  • Avoid applying a suspected new product again until you can get appropriate advice.
  • Do not use this website to select medication or diagnose a condition.
  • Ask a parent/guardian or trusted adult to help arrange medical advice if you are concerned.
  • Do not upload identifiable or private medical images to an educational prototype.
Why no allergy prediction?

The current lesion dataset is not labelled for allergy diagnosis. A rash image alone cannot reliably establish the cause, and this prototype is not clinically validated.
